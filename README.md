* **Transuranic Dwarf Mono-Biomes:** Small-scale planetary bodies (radius 200–500 km) maintain a steady 1g surface gravity due to ultra-dense cores composed of stabilized 9th-period elements (**Hofitum-125**, **Unbiniilium-120**).
* **Flowing Greenhouse Equilibrium:** Dwarf worlds lack a global magnetosphere due to tectonic solidification (Post-Tectonic Phenomenon), yet their atmospheres remain safe from stellar wind stripping due to a self-regulating, dynamic mass-flux valve at the exosphere boundary.

### 3. Macro-Engineering & Energy Harvesting (Stage 1-3)
* **Planetary Induction Rings:** Macro-loops deployed in geostationary orbits harvest the planet's internal dynamo flux, transmitting terahertz energy down to isolated industrial rectenna fields.
* **Structural Induction:** Urban centers and starship chassis are completely devoid of physical wiring. Energy is safely distributed within the superconducting metallic "skeleton" of structures via high-frequency skin effects and accessed through near-field magnetic resonance, rendering it entirely imperceptible and safe for organic contact.
* **TER Matrices (Tensor Electrodynamic Receivers):** Heavy stationary cores interact directly with subquantum string tension utilizing **Resonant Charoite** matrices, bypassing thermal or mechanical transduction stages.
* **Subatomic Flywheels:** High-output portable power systems exploit the immense inertial moment of ultra-heavy crystalline elements, functioning as explosion-proof, non-volatile, relativistically weight-variant capacitors ($E=mc^2$).

### 4. Molecular Biology & Evolutionary Dynamics (Stage 0-3)
* **Dismantling of Radiation Defenses:** Organisms lack energy-intensive repair proteins (p53, ATM kinase), redirecting 20–30% of their metabolic budget toward lifespan extension, cognitive amplification, and exotic evolutionary hybridization (**Mammal Plant / Fustix** lineages).
* **Endogenous Mutagenesis:** Genetic diversification does not rely on chaotic ionizing radiation. Evolutionary adaptation is driven by hyper-active chromosomal crossing-over and stress-triggered transposable elements (transposons), resulting in rapid, directed, and adaptive mutagenic responses.
* **Bio-Phonon Photo-Reception:** Visual organs utilize quartz-fluorite biological lenses and metal-organic resonance antennas, completely bypassing chemical pigment bleaching.
